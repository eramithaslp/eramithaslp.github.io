    Technology in Audiology

    Full lecture notes for every topic in the departmental technology teaching plan, plus supplementary topics on modulation, AI for speech and audio, large language models, clinic power systems and clean power, hearing-aid system-on-chip design, and a full unit on clinical audiology instruments. Each topic explains what the technology is, why audiologists need it, how it works, its architecture and signal flow, components, algorithms, clinical applications, limitations, demonstrations and current developments, with worked examples and interactive demonstrations.
