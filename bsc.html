    Electronics and Acoustics

    Lecture notes for the B.Sc. ASLP paper B2.4 Electronics and Acoustics (45 hours, 3 credits). The notes start from first principles and cover every syllabus item: basic electronics and power supplies, the physics of sound and room acoustics, transducers, sound recording and digital signal processing, information technology, and the instruments used in speech and hearing, including audiometer calibration. Each topic has diagrams, worked examples, interactive demonstrations, key points for revision and exam-style exercises with model answers.

    46 topics 5 units 45 hours Interactive demonstrations
    01

    Units and topics

    After completing this course the student will be able to identify the types of power supply for clinics and biomedical instruments, understand the basic aspects of digital signal processing and speech processing, state the theoretical basis of acoustics required for audiologists, understand the basic aspects of information technology, and analyse the principles of working of the major instruments used in assessment.

    Unit 1

    Introduction to Electronics and Signals

    Resistors, capacitors, inductors, transformers, diodes, transistors and amplifiers; DC and AC power supplies, earthing, isolation transformers and UPS; number systems, analogue and digital signals, ADC and DAC.

    10 topics: 1.1, 1.2, 1.3, 1.4, 1.5, 1.6, 1.7, 1.8, 1.9, 1.10    Unit 2

    Introduction to Acoustics

    Physics of sound, the decibel, loudness and equal loudness contours; reflection, absorption, reverberation, background noise and acoustically treated rooms; microphones, loudspeakers, earphones and receivers.

    10 topics: 2.1, 2.2, 2.3, 2.4, 2.5, 2.6, 2.7, 2.8, 2.9, 2.10    Unit 3

    Sound Recording, Signal Representation and DSP

    Time domain, frequency domain and spectrogram; systems and software for sound recording; structure of a digital processing system, analogue vs digital processing, DSP applications, and time and frequency domain speech processing.

    9 topics: 3.1, 3.2, 3.3, 3.4, 3.5, 3.6, 3.7, 3.8, 3.9    Unit 4

    Introduction to Information Technology

    Computer hardware and specifications, operating systems, application software for communication sciences and disorders, mobile apps, the internet and WWW, local area networks, tele-diagnosis and tele-rehabilitation.

    8 topics: 4.1, 4.2, 4.3, 4.4, 4.5, 4.6, 4.7, 4.8    Unit 5

    Instrumentation in Speech and Hearing

    Pre-amplifiers, power amplifiers and filters; digital hearing aids, group amplification and assistive listening devices, audiometers, middle ear analysers, speech and voice analysis systems; calibration equipment and audiometer calibration.

    9 topics: 5.1, 5.2, 5.3, 5.4, 5.5, 5.6, 5.7, 5.8, 5.9
